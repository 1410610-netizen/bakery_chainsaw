Client-side website outline.

        
The website is designed to showcase bakery products and services, collect customer enquiries, promote social media channels and provide contact details. It uses HTML for structure, CSS for styling and JavaScript for the menu, form message and cake calculator.

        
The site covers the assignment requirements: order form, marketing opt-out, cake customisation calculator, product information, photo spaces, social links, external recipe links, reviews, location, contact information and business hours.
